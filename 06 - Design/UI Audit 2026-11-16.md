# UI/UX audit, 2026-11-16

Based on screenshots of the arrangement, palette, welcome, and Concept Guide.
Related: [[Track Colors]], [[01 - Vision and Philosophy]].

## 1. Pro engineer (Logic-trained)
- **Track headers are wasteful and don't feel like an instrument.** System sliders with a "Pan" text
  label and a "+0.0 dB" readout take 96 pt per track. There's no pan knob and no proper fader feel
  (taper, value readout while dragging, ⌥-click reset).
- **Meters are hidden.** A 5 pt bar under the pan slider can't be read at a glance. Pros expect a
  vertical meter at the header's edge, like Logic's track header meter.
- **M and S are generic text buttons.** On-states don't follow pro conventions: mute should desaturate
  the track, solo should glow yellow.
- **The control bar wastes height.** The transport sits *below* an empty 28 pt title bar. Logic merges
  them.
- **No track numbers.** It's hard to talk about "track 7" with a collaborator.

## 2. Visual designer
- **Hierarchy is flat.** Everything is 10–12 pt and the same weight. The time display is the only
  focal point, but the amber accent is also used for meters, sliders, and the playhead, so nothing
  stands out.
- **Clip title bars are fully saturated slabs** with dark text. They dominate the waveforms, which are
  the actual content. Region headers should be quiet. The waveform is the hero.
- **Colors are arbitrary.** Eight hand-picked hues with uneven lightness: the teal reads darker than the
  amber, so tracks look unequal in importance.
- **The status line is a string of facts joined by "·".** It's dense, low value, and reads like debug output.
- **Spacing doesn't follow one grid.** Mixed 3/4/6/8/10/12 pt insets and 1 px separators at different
  opacities.

## 3. First-time user
- **The empty state is two centered lines.** It needs a real drop target and the three actions people
  actually want (Import Audio, Open Project, Bring Projects from Another DAW).
- **Nothing hints at the gestures:** fade handles, ⌥-drag stretch, ⌃ snap bypass. Hover affordances
  should teach them.

## 4. Accessibility
- **Equal-lightness colors fail color-blind users** (about 8% of men). Adjacent tracks need a lightness
  difference as well as a hue difference.
- Custom controls must expose VoiceOver roles, values, and increment and decrement actions, and support
  the keyboard.
- **Contrast:** dim text (#8A8F98 on #1B1D21) is about 5:1, which is fine. Clip-title text on saturated
  colors is unchecked. It should pick black or white by luminance.

## 5. "AI-generated" tells to remove
- Stock AppKit controls dropped into a custom-drawn canvas (two visual languages at once).
- One accent color on everything. Uniform rounded rectangles. Labels that state the obvious ("Pan",
  "TRACKS").
- Decorative separators ("·"), centered helper text, and status sentences instead of instrumentation.
- No micro-detail: hairlines, inner shadows, pressed states, hover states, value bubbles. Craft lives here.

## Changes in this pass (priority order)
1. **Track color system** ([[Track Colors]]): a perceptually uniform OKLCH gradient starting at green,
   with lightness alternating for color-blind separation, per-track custom colors, and inheritance from a
   colored group or section. Clips, waveforms, meters, and headers derive from it.
2. **New track header:**
   - number, color strip, name, and M/S chips with pro state colors
   - custom fader with an audio taper, value bubble, and ⌥-click/double-click to reset
   - rotary pan knob
   - vertical stereo meter on the header's edge
   - VoiceOver and keyboard support
3. **Clips:** a quiet title strip (tinted, not solid), contrast-aware text, a waveform in a lighter
   shade of the track color, a crisp 1 px border, and a clear selected state. Muted clips are hatched.
4. **Unified control bar:** the transport moves into the title bar. A centered LCD shows the position
   in large type, with the project name and audio status as secondary text. Grouped transport buttons
   and a master meter. The status sentence is gone.
5. **Ruler and playhead:** a playhead cap in the ruler, quieter ticks, and labels aligned to their ticks.
6. **Empty state:** a drop zone with three actions.
7. **Tokens:** a 4 pt spacing grid, a type scale (11/12/13/15/22), and one hairline color.
