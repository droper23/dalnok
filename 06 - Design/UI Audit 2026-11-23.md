# UI audit 2026-11-23: every look, every screen

The owner: "a wide-scope UI/UX audit and critique across every single theme and aspect of the app … from a variety of
perspectives", with Graphite, Warm Analog and Prism raised to Atelier's level and made "more visually impressive and
distinct", Atelier pushed further, "an abundance of high-quality animations", and iterations of review → improvement →
review, ending "spectacular, immersive, visually outstanding … while remaining reliable and fast".

Method: gallery stills of all six looks (`--gallery`, scale 2, the generated band project, plug-in chain of built-in and
Apple units), reviewed by independent reviewers, each from one perspective. Round 1 (seven reviewers): Graphite (product
designer), Warm Analog (hardware-studio veteran), Prism (motion/visual designer), workflow (producer and mix engineer),
accessibility and first-time user, Atelier visual (editorial/scientific illustration), Atelier code (AppKit craft and
speed). Round 2 (five): one per look again, judging round 1's changes, plus motion and interaction. Each finding was
checked against the stills before it was acted on.

## What the reviews found (the main points)
- **Stock macOS controls** (blue check boxes, popups, sliders, default buttons, text selection) in every look: the one
  complaint every reviewer made.
- **Graphite, Warm Analog and Prism had no figures of their own:** Prism was Graphite under glass; the plug-in screens
  were static; only Atelier's diagrams moved and linked to their parameters.
- **Empty states:** the start page showed the mixer's Master strip through it and a dashed frame round an empty page;
  the empty device chain was a lone grey card in a dead slab.
- **Feedback:** a solo didn't show which tracks it silenced; "muted" was written only in two looks; the master meters
  differed in colour logic and were hard to read.
- **Warm Analog:** pedal knob labels truncated into duplicates ("LOW CU…" ×4); the sidechain a system popup.
- **Settings:** a flat list that grew one feature at a time; Graphite-only rows shown in every look.
- **First launch after a crash:** the recovery sheet opened over the main window and hid the welcome window.
- **Speed:** Atelier's figure clock redrew every frame even when the app was in the background or a figure scrolled
  out of view; the needle meter redrew at 60 Hz in silence.

## What changed (rounds 1 and 2)
- **Figures for every look** (`LookFigures.swift`, `PedalScreen.swift`), same parameters, handles, hover links and motion
  as Atelier's, each in its own idiom:
  - Graphite "telemetry": phosphor traces with glow on a dot grid, amber handles: gain computer, decay field, echo taps,
    transfer, LFO scope.
  - Prism "optics": spectral light on the glass: aperture (an iris that closes as it reduces), caustics, light echoes,
    refraction, interference; the EQ's curve and analyzer coloured by frequency ("dispersion").
  - Warm Analog: each pedal's window draws its circuit, moving: tape loop with heads and echo bars, a swaying spring, a
    triode's filament and the clipped wave, a lamp over the LFO; compressor a gain-reduction needle; EQ a glowing curve.
- **Controls in the look:** check boxes drawn by the look (`Theme.checkbox`, Atelier's saltire), popups as framed wells
  (Atelier ruled boxes), push buttons on the look's face, the default button and slider fills in its colour, text
  selection tinted (`Theme.tintControls`, applied as windows become key and after model changes).
- **Settings:** nine pages (General, Appearance, Audio, MIDI, Recording, Plug-ins, Keyboard Shortcuts, Files and Library,
  Advanced) with a search field; the Library's folders and preview, the device panel height and recovery/recordings
  folders moved in; rack-only rows show only in Graphite; the selection glides; pages arrive in the look's motion.
- **Prism's secondary windows** (Settings, first launch) are glass over the light field.
- **Empty device chain** is a figure per look (an empty rack slot on a trace, an unpatched cable and a ghost pedal, a beam
  through an empty glass card) with a point of light travelling the signal path; clicking the slot adds a plug-in.
- **Start page:** the mixer is hidden with the side panels; the drop zone appears only while files are dragged over.
- **Feedback:** a solo fades every lane it silences (as the engine routes solo, through buses), crossfading as it
  changes; "muted" in words in every look; per-look meter ramps (Graphite teal→amber→coral, Warm Analog LED
  green→amber→red, Prism cyan→violet→pink); Graphite and Warm Analog's playhead leaves an afterglow while it runs.
- **Warm Analog:** pedal labels shortened the way panels print them (LC FREQ, LS GAIN, THRESH, A GAIN); the sidechain is
  a patch cable in the source track's colour into an SC jack.
- **Plug-ins:** built-ins read "Built in", not "Not scanned"; Library rows show VST3/AU/CLAP so two formats of one
  plug-in no longer look like duplicates; "Favourites" spelt one way.
- **First launch:** the crash-recovery offer waits until the welcome questions are answered or closed; closing the
  window counts as answered (Settings ▸ General ▸ Choose Look and Shortcuts Again…).
- **Speed:** the figure clock holds while Dalnok is in the background, minimised, covered or scrolled out of view; the
  needle meter redraws only when its reading changes. `--perf 20` on the 64-track project, Prism Dark and Light: 0
  overloads, 0 missed blocks, main thread p99 8 ms, worst pass 20 ms.
- **Atelier:** Fig. 4's output wave stays inside the plate, "in"/"out" clear of the strokes; sample values in the
  figures gallery instead of "value 1".

## How it was checked
Gallery stills of all six looks after each round (looked at, not assumed); `DALNOK_GALLERY_MOTION=1` steps every figure
on the device panel through its motion and writes a frame per quarter second (06-motion-01…08), so each animation's
drawing was checked frame by frame; `DALNOK_GALLERY_SETTINGS=1` writes every Settings page and a search. Live, on-screen
capture of the animations wasn't possible: the screen was locked through the session. Self-test steps added: Settings'
nine pages and search, the solo-silenced set, the recovery offer waiting for first launch.

## Still open (round 3 and later)
- Graphite: replace the brushed-metal faceplate around the new figures with a flat telemetry card (reviewer's biggest
  point: the signature is still in 5 % of the pixels); value arcs on knobs; a level sparkline per track header.
- Warm Analog: console strip furniture (machined R/M/S keys with lenses, aluminium fader caps), twin VU meters for the
  master in the bar, a studio-desk start page with an idling tape deck.
- Prism: a spectral master meter chip; frosted mixer meter wells; the plug-in browser, palette and piano roll as glass;
  the light version's contrast pass (yellow and cyan beams under 2:1).
- Atelier: drawn sliders and scrollers; a hatched-ink piano roll; a window-wide neatline with plate numerals; wet-ink
  recording; the Dark "Tracks" band to the divider (V3); C7, C12.
- All: per-look panel open/close timing (`Motion.character`), knobs sweeping to new values on preset load, track delete
  collapsing its lane, list selection glides elsewhere (palette, plug-in list, Library); accessibility (focus rings on
  custom controls, fader legends ≥ 4.5:1, Increase Contrast); the arrangement toolbar items producers expect (snap/grid,
  zoom, follow); a CPU/latency readout; inserts listed on mixer strips; the Composition Overview showing every track.
