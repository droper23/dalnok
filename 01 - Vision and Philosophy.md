# Vision and Philosophy

**Professional functionality, excellent usability, and exceptional efficiency are not mutually exclusive.**

## Who it's for first
The primary user today is a Logic Pro user. These are their bars for switching:
- **Must match or beat:** comping and take folders, Flex Time and Flex Pitch-style editing, stock
  instruments and effects, track stacks, and the mixer.
- **Must clearly beat:**
  1. Openness: fully scriptable, with macros, custom actions, and a stable API.
  2. Discoverability: no hidden menus. A command palette and context-sensitive controls.
  3. Ableton-like flexibility, with clip launching and timeline on one track model.
  4. Every plugin parameter editable in a generic in-DAW panel, without opening the plugin's own window.

## Principles
1. **Recording integrity outranks everything.**
2. **Performance is a feature.** We measure it and never claim it without numbers.
3. **One coherent app.** Workspaces (Record, Compose, Mix, Master, Dialogue, Perform) are views, not modes.
4. **Low interaction cost.** Frequent tasks take the fewest actions, and everything is keyboard-reachable.
5. **Pro-first visuals with many options.** A refined graphite default, plus a high-end analog theme family.
6. **Local and private.** Works fully offline. Intelligent features are optional, local, and come later.
7. **Independent architecture.** Our own engine, model, and UI, built on permissive libraries.

The test for every decision: *does it make the DAW faster, more capable, more intuitive, more beautiful,
more reliable, or easier to extend?*
