# 2026-11-23

**UI audit of every look** (`06 - Design/UI Audit 2026-11-23.md`). The owner asked for a wide critique from several
perspectives, then iterations of review and improvement, with Graphite, Warm Analog and Prism brought to Atelier's level.

- Rendered all six looks (`--gallery`) and ran seven reviewers on them (one per look, workflow, accessibility and
  newcomer, Atelier's code). Their common finding: stock blue controls everywhere, and the three older looks had no
  figures of their own.
- Built figures for Graphite (telemetry) and Prism (optics) on Atelier's figure framework, and moving circuits in Warm
  Analog's pedal windows. `FigureClock` now drives any view; it also holds when the app is in the background or the
  figure is scrolled away, which the code reviewer found it didn't.
- Settings regrouped into nine pages with search; stock controls drawn in each look. A class-swap experiment for buttons
  crashed (Swift subclass swapped onto an AppKit instance) and was dropped for bezel colours.
- Round 2 (five reviewers) judged round 1: figures landed in all three looks; still-static EQ and compressor in Warm
  Analog, EQ in Prism, popups and buttons were the next fixes, all done.
- Found by using the app: after the forced quit last night, the crash-recovery sheet hid the first-launch window. The
  offer now waits for the first-launch questions.
- The screen stayed locked all session, so animations were checked by stepping them offscreen
  (`DALNOK_GALLERY_MOTION`) rather than by live capture.

**Afternoon: owner's follow-ups after 0.4.0.**
- Reverted the Graphite and Warm Analog plug-in figures (owner preferred the old ones); Atelier lost its crop marks
  between plug-ins and gained folding units and a wider OPEN box (PR #18).
- The self-test matrix took ~14 min run one look at a time. Running copies in parallel failed at first because they
  shared the "Dalnok" preferences domain; renaming each copy's binary gives it its own domain. `CFFIXED_USER_HOME`
  didn't isolate preferences, and halving waits broke real-time steps. Steps that slept after seeks now wait on state.
- Atelier compressor rebuilt as a two-pen seismograph (the owner chose it over two other concepts), and key controls
  picked per unit for readability; a sextant for amounts at the owner's request. Overlap pass on every figure. Plain
  dials setting added for anyone who prefers ordinary knobs (PR #19).
- Background test runs added Dictation and Emoji items to the Edit menu (accessory policy), which broke the shortcut
  collision check; disabled them under `DALNOK_BACKGROUND`.
