# 2026-11-21

## First run and motion pass
- Owner brief: appealing to new users, simple to navigate but powerful, and more animation, informed by other DAWs and creative apps. Research table and decisions: `06 - Design/First Run and Motion 2026-11-21.md`.
- Start page (replaces the five-button empty state):
  - Six templates, each one undo step ("New Make a Beat Project").
  - Five recent projects. They are never shown in snapshot, screenshot, self-test, UI-walk or tour runs, so the owner's project names can't land in a picture.
  - Other ways in, and three tips. The sections rise in a 45 ms stagger.
- Control bar view buttons, with a new `IconButton.isActive` (an accent pill eased in and out).
  - The launcher now slides open like the mixer.
- Hint line: one app-wide mouse-moved monitor walks from the view under the pointer up to the first tooltip or accessibility help.
  - The timeline supplies zone-aware text.
  - Messages (`flash`) keep priority for their 4 s.
- `ToastView`: undo/redo/save and templates; a repeat nudges instead of re-entering.
- `Motion.drive` adds a display-linked 0→1 driver for content drawn in `draw(_:)`. `TimelineView.glide` uses it for zoom and scroll.
  - The zoom eases log(frames per point) around the fixed point of the two views. The anchor drifted under 0.5 pt in the self-test.
  - Page turns while playing glide; gestures cancel a glide.
- Lanes: new tracks glow in their colour (0.8 s), but not on project open. Track or clip mute cross-fades by fading a picture of the old clips (0.22 s).
- Count-in overlay; palette grows from 97 %; theme switch cross-fades from a cached picture of the old window.
- Self-test: new `SelfTest+FirstRun.swift`.
  - First run: one failure, the hint check expecting the status line while a launcher message held it, which is by design. The check now allows for that.
  - Final: 614 checks (Graphite, 48 kHz) and 599 (Warm Analog, 44.1 kHz), both PASS.
