## Tempo and meter maps (Phase 3 closed)
- Kept MIDI stored in frames and made every tempo edit a remap through beats (old map → new map) rather than migrating
  the model to beat-domain storage. This keeps every edit path, the C API and the UI unchanged, and handles the cases
  the earlier review flagged: notes crossing a change map each endpoint separately, and a note list shared by clips
  at different origins is split per origin while split pieces (same origin) keep sharing. Cost: up to one frame of
  rounding per tempo edit.
- Metronome clicks are scheduled from tempo and meter segments in the RenderState (no libm on the audio thread).
- The control bar edits the tempo/meter segment under the playhead; Transport menu adds and removes changes.
- Marquee clip selection finished and committed from the previous session's work in progress.
- Open: count-in, tempo ramps, tempo-map import, sample-accurate MIDI input, MiniLab test.
